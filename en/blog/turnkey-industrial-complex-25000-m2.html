Corporate · Grupo Sánchez · Hidalgo · 25,000 m²

Industrial Complex: Designed for Productivity and Wellbeing

At Grupo Caabsa Steel México we believe a workplace should be not only functional, but inspiring. That is why, in our turnkey project, we developed an office center that combines modernity, comfort and efficiency, as part of an industrial complex of more than 25,000 square meters.

This integral project includes not only a first-class administrative building, but also production areas, warehouses, silo installations, truck scales, internal roadways and service areas — all designed to optimize our client's operations and drive their growth.

Offices that inspire: a space designed around people

The new office center is much more than a place to work. It is an environment designed to encourage collaboration, focus and the wellbeing of the administrative team.

What you will find in this building:

  • Spacious, bright administrative areas: Designed with open-office concepts that favor communication and flexibility.
  • Meeting and conference rooms: Spaces equipped with state-of-the-art technology for executive meetings, presentations and video conferencing.
  • Break and service areas: Areas designed for staff comfort, promoting a balance between productivity and wellbeing.

Every corner has been carefully planned to offer a superior work experience, with quality finishes, natural light and an intelligent layout.

Industrial warehouse: efficiency and capacity at scale

The operational heart of the complex features a large industrial warehouse and state-of-the-art production areas. These spaces were designed to:

  • Maximize storage capacity.
  • Guarantee safety at all times.
  • Facilitate internal logistics and material flow.

The integration of technology and functional design means every square meter works in favor of productivity.

Technology and heavy infrastructure: high-performance silos and truck scales

One of the great challenges of this project was incorporating specialized installations for four industrial silos and a truck weighing system. These structures not only increase production and storage capacity, they also optimize loading, unloading and raw material control.

Key benefits:

  • Precision in weighing and inventory control.
  • Faster receipt and dispatch of materials.
  • Reduced downtime in the operation.

Internal mobility: strategic roadways and service areas

To guarantee a smooth, safe operation, the complex features perfectly laid out internal roadways that allow efficient transit of heavy and light vehicles. Service areas have also been strategically placed to facilitate daily maintenance and logistics.

An integral project, a total solution

This development is a clear example of how a turnkey solution can completely transform a company's operating capacity. From the foundation to the final architectural details, every element was executed to the highest quality standards.

At Caabsa Steel México, we build spaces that drive businesses forward.
